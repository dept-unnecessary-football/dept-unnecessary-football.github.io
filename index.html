The Department of Unnecessary Football

Football, explained badly. Researched somewhat better.

Departments

Each division covers one part of football.

  1. SUB

    Subcommittee on Usage and Benches

    Who plays, who sits, and whether the bench was ever as deep as advertised.

  2. FLOP

    Fees, Losses and Overpayments Panel

    Values every transfer twice: on the day it is announced, and on the day it is quietly forgotten.

  3. TABLE

    Tribunal for Asymmetry, Balance and League Equality

    Hears complaints that the league was decided in August. Measures before ruling.

  4. ODDS

    Office of Disputed Decimal Speculation

    Reads the betting markets so you don’t have to. Considers the bookmaker’s margin a primary source.

  5. BAD

    Bureau of Armband Decisions

    Makes one decision a week under uncertainty, records it before the deadline, and audits it afterwards. Takes the hits so the Department doesn’t have to.

  6. SACK

    Standing Authority on Caretakers and Knee-jerks

    Keeps the clock on every manager. Records the vote of confidence, then the sacking, then the bounce, if any.
