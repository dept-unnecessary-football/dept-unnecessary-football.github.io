Table Manners: how competitive are the top five leagues?

Competitive balance index for the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1, this season against every season since 2016-17. Weekly CSV.

How it's measured

Definitions. Matchweek k uses each team’s first k league matches. Noll-Scully ratio: the standard deviation of points across teams divided by its value in a league of equal teams with the same draw rate (1 = as even as chance allows; higher = less balanced). The equal-teams band is the 2.5th to 97.5th percentile of that ratio in 2,000 simulated equal-team seasons: early in a season, a value inside it cannot be told apart from luck. HICB = 100 × number of teams × the sum of squared points shares (100 = equal shares). Gap 1st–4th per game is in points. The percentile ranks this season against the ten seasons since 2016-17 at the same matchweek; with ten seasons it moves in steps of ten and is a rank, not a test.

Coverage. 18,437 matches in 55 league-seasons, 2016-17 to 2026-27 (500 in 2026-27); 2026-27 complete to matchweek: Premier League 10, LaLiga 11, Serie A 11, Bundesliga 9, Ligue 1 10 (every team has played at least that many) (updated 2026-11-16).
