Against the Odds: football betting odds, upsets and calibration

How often the bookmakers' favourite wins, the week's biggest upsets and team strength ratings from closing odds. Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1. Weekly CSV.

How it's measured

Definitions. Probabilities come from the market-average closing price for home win, draw and away win, with the bookmaker’s margin removed proportionally (each implied probability divided by their sum). Shin’s method, which moves probability from outsiders to favourites, is published alongside as a check: results that rest on odds can change with the method used. A surprise is a result the market gave a low probability. Ratings are in log-odds: a gap of 0.5 between two teams means the market rated the stronger one’s chance of beating the other at about 1.65 times the reverse, venue aside; the standard error shows how consistently the market priced a team, not a forecast range. Calibration groups every predicted probability into tenths and compares it with how often the outcome happened (Wilson 95% intervals). A match gives three linked predictions, so the intervals are a little too narrow.

Coverage. 2026-27: 500 matches in the five leagues, 2026-08-15 to 2026-11-08; the week: 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-08; comparison: 12,459 matches, 2019-20 to 2025-26. Mean bookmaker margin in the closing average 6.1% (2026-27) (updated 2026-11-16).
