Minutes of the Meeting: Premier League squad rotation and minutes

How many players each Premier League club really uses: effective squad size, rotation and minutes share, updated weekly. Free CSV.

How it's measured

Definitions. Effective squad size is the Hill number of each club’s league-minutes shares: q=1 (exponential of Shannon entropy) and q=2 (inverse Simpson). With eleven players on the pitch the floor is 11: a club that used the same eleven for every minute scores exactly 11. It grows with the number of matches, so the comparison is always with the same number of matches in earlier seasons. XI changed per match is the average share of the starting eleven replaced from one league match to the next, with its standard error over the transitions. Top-11 share is the share of a club’s minutes played by its eleven most-used players. Minutes are capped at 90 per player per match. These describe how minutes were shared, not squad quality, injuries or a manager’s intentions.

Coverage. Premier League 2026-27, gameweeks 1 to 10 (20 clubs, 100 matches, 10 per club); context: 60 club-seasons 2022-23 to 2024-25, each club's first 10 league matches (updated 2026-11-16).
