Departments

The Department is organised into divisions. Jurisdictions overlap. Disputes are settled in proceedings.

  1. Div. 01

    Department of Squadology

    Who plays, who doesn’t, and whether the squad was ever as deep as the manager said.

  2. Div. 02

    Office of Transfer Affairs

    Fees, flows, and the ongoing search for what “Premier League proven” means.

  3. Div. 03

    Department of Competitive Complaints

    Hears grievances that the league is rigged, boring, or decided in August. Occasionally upholds one.

  4. Div. 04

    Bureau of Small Samples

    Reviews every conclusion drawn from four matches. Rejects most of them.

  5. Div. 05

    Department of Things That Probably Aren’t Significant

    Keeps the register of patterns that disappear inside a confidence interval.

  6. Div. 06

    Department of Extremely Normal Spending

    Audits wages, fees and net spend. Finds it all extremely normal.

  7. Div. 07

    Department of Graphs Nobody Asked For

    Produces the chart anyway.

  8. Div. 08

    Office of Unforeseen Consequences

    Rule changes, and what they did instead of what they were for.

  9. Div. 09

    Department of Football Metaphysics

    Is form real? What is a big game? The questions other departments refuse to take.

  10. Div. 10

    Office of Things We Tried and Regretted

    Keeps the failed models, so nobody has to fail at them twice.
