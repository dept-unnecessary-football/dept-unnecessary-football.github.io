About the Department

Some football questions don’t need a government inquiry. They get one anyway.

The Department of Unnecessary Football takes the questions fans already argue about and investigates them with methods nobody asked for. Then it explains the answer so that anyone who cared about the question can follow it.

Why football

Football is a laboratory that doesn’t know it’s a laboratory. Markets, incentives, labour, uncertainty and institutions, all running every weekend, all in public, all with a scoreboard.

The subject stays football. The machinery is just unusually serious.

How the Department works

  • Intuition, then picture, then number, then method. In that order.
  • Every finding carries its uncertainty. Especially the inconvenient kind.
  • Exploratory is labelled exploratory. No correlation is promoted to cause without a hearing.
  • Every number is rebuilt from data. Nothing is typed in by hand.
  • Failed investigations are filed, not shredded.

What to expect

Proceedings, mostly. Some end in a chart, some in a paper, some in a short note explaining why the question was harder than it looked. All are admissible.

The Department regrets nothing. The Office of Things We Tried and Regretted has filed a dissent.
